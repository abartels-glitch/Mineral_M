FEOC Compliance Passport (MVP)

Review queue Passport lookup Scan

Scan a part's Data Matrix code

Point a camera at the Data Matrix printed on a part (generated at credential issuance — see index.html) and it resolves straight to that credential's passport, no login required. Uses ZXing, vendored locally under frontend/vendor/ rather than a CDN, to keep the frontend self-contained like the rest of the app.

Not live-camera-tested in development — built against ZXing's documented BrowserMultiFormatReader API; verify this yourself with a real device/camera.
